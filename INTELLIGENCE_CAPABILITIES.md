- Propaganda pattern recognition across state media sources

## GEOINT Coverage (8 Regions)

### Middle East — CRITICAL
- **Countries**: Iran, Iraq, Syria, Israel, Palestine (Gaza/West Bank), Lebanon, Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman
- **Key Flashpoints**: Iran nuclear program, Gaza conflict, Lebanon-Israel border, Yemen civil war, Houthi Red Sea attacks, Strait of Hormuz
- **Indicators Monitored**: Military mobilization, energy infrastructure, maritime traffic anomalies, proxy force activity

### Eastern Europe — HIGH
- **Countries**: Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Moldova, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan + NATO Eastern Flank (Poland, Baltic States, Romania)
- **Key Flashpoints**: Ukraine-Russia conflict, NATO-Russia tensions, Transnistria, Nagorno-Karabakh
- **Indicators Monitored**: Force disposition, logistics movement, electronic warfare, energy infrastructure

### Africa — HIGH
- **Countries**: Nigeria, Ethiopia, Somalia, Sudan, Libya, Mali, Burkina Faso, Mozambique, DRC, Central African Republic, Chad
- **Key Flashpoints**: Sahel insurgencies, Horn of Africa piracy, DRC eastern conflict, Libya civil war, Sudan civil war
- **Indicators Monitored**: Non-state armed group activity, civilian displacement, Wagner/mercenary presence

### Southeast Asia — HIGH
- **Countries**: Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, Myanmar, Cambodia, Thailand, Singapore, Brunei
- **Key Flashpoints**: South China Sea territorial disputes, Spratly/Paracel Islands, Myanmar civil war, Mekong resource competition
- **Indicators Monitored**: Maritime patrol activity, island-building, diplomatic posturing

### East Asia — MODERATE
- **Countries**: China, Taiwan, North Korea, South Korea, Japan
- **Key Flashpoints**: Taiwan Strait, Korean Peninsula, Senkaku/Diaoyu Islands, DPRK nuclear/missile program
- **Indicators Monitored**: PLA exercises, DPRK launch activity, economic coercion signals

### South Asia — MODERATE
- **Countries**: India, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal
- **Key Flashpoints**: India-Pakistan Line of Control (Kashmir), Afghanistan stability, India-China border (LAC)
- **Indicators Monitored**: LoC ceasefire violations, Taliban governance indicators, India-China patrol activity

### Americas — MODERATE
- **Countries**: Venezuela, Colombia, Mexico, Haiti, Cuba, Nicaragua, Guatemala, Honduras, El Salvador
- **Key Flashpoints**: Venezuela political instability, Colombia FARC remnants, Mexican cartel violence, Haiti gang control, Cuba political unrest
- **Indicators Monitored**: Cartel territorial control, migration crisis indicators, political instability
