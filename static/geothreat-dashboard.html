72-Hour Global Threat Forecast?Piecewise linear trend + Fourier seasonality model (Prophet-style) fitted to hourly event counts over the past 7 days. The shaded band shows the 80% confidence interval. Forecast degrades in accuracy beyond 48h. Driven entirely by real ingested events — no synthetic data.

Regional Risk Assessment?Per-region composite risk score (0–100) derived from three signals: (1) 48h event rate vs. baseline, (2) weighted severity of recent events (critical×4, high×2, moderate×1), (3) source diversity (more corroborating outlets = higher confidence). Scores normalised across all active regions.

Confidence Decay?Forecast confidence decreases over the horizon. Starts at the model's in-sample accuracy (typically 95–99%) and decays exponentially as uncertainty compounds. At 72h the confidence floor reflects the model's out-of-sample performance on held-out data. Use forecasts beyond 48h as indicative, not predictive.
